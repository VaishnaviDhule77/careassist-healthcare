Key Features

Built for Real-World Impact

Every feature addresses a genuine accessibility barrier faced by elderly users and their caregivers.

Emergency SOS

One-tap button grabs GPS and instantly alerts all emergency contacts with live location on Google Maps.

Smart Medication

Shows only the next pill to take. Large green button confirms. No complex schedules to read.

Voice Assistant

Ask health questions by speaking. AI responds in your language and reads answers aloud.

Multilingual

Full UI, speech recognition, and text-to-speech in English, Hindi, and Marathi.

Zero-Input Modes

Auto-scanning with switch control, sound triggers, and webcam gesture support for severe motor disabilities.

Caregiver Analytics

Adherence rates, vitals history, missed dose flags, and complete activity logging in real-time.

How It Works

End-to-End Flow

From patient action to caregiver awareness in seconds.

1

Patient Acts

Taps a button, speaks, or uses a switch to take medicine or trigger SOS

2

App Processes

Grabs GPS, logs the action, updates schedule, and queues notifications

3

Caregiver Notified

Dashboard updates in real-time. SOS triggers immediate alert with map link

4

AI Assists

Voice AI answers patient questions and summarizes symptoms for the doctor
